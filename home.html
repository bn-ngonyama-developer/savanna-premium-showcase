About Savanna

Savanna is a premium cider brand that has been a staple in South Africa for decades. Known for its crisp and refreshing taste, Savanna offers a variety of flavors that cater to different palates. From the classic Savanna Dry to the bold Angry Lemon, there's a Savanna for every occasion.

Our Mission

At Savanna, we are committed to delivering high-quality beverages that bring people together. Our mission is to create memorable experiences through our products, while promoting responsible drinking and sustainability in our production processes.
